Duck vs. Lava: Taco Catch is a quick, casual browser game for students and anyone who wants a two-minute break without installing anything. The problem it solves is simple: people want a short, funny distraction that works on a laptop or phone and doesn’t need an account or download. The player controls a rubber duck standing on a floor that is slowly turning to lava. Tacos fall from the sky, and the player slides the duck left and right (arrow keys, A/D, or dragging a finger on a phone) to catch them. Every missed taco makes the lava rise, and if the lava gets too high, the game ends. As the score climbs, the tacos fall faster and appear more often. The twist is that the game gives the player a silly, over-the-top compliment every few seconds (“Scientists agree: you are extremely buoyant”), so even a losing run feels good. The player’s goal is to catch as many tacos as possible and beat their own saved best score.
